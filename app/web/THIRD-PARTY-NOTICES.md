The Angular application foundation is adapted from the Ionic Team starter at
https://github.com/ionic-team/starters/tree/15aee8b93f46b48e4db7464bceb4b0560ea5dc35/angular-standalone.
The standalone Angular base supplies the workspace configuration and bootstrap;
the official sidemenu example supplies the split-pane shell structure. Demo
routes, labels, and sample content were removed. The Sygnity logo and favicon
are local repository assets selected for this internal PoC and are not part of
the Ionic starter.
